## 매트 세라믹 3D

결과: [matte-3d.png](matte-3d.png)

최종 생성 프롬프트:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a coordinated three-icon concept board for the COMMECT corporate coffee business website.
Primary request: Create a premium, coherent set of THREE sculptural illustrations, arranged left to right in ONE spacious horizontal row. The same three concepts must read clearly and be equal in visual size.
Subjects, left to right:
1. Global Network: an elegant globe made of a few latitude/longitude bands, with three small connection nodes attached around it. The globe conveys international business connections, not shipping logistics.
2. Market Understanding: two offset document panels and a magnifying glass. Inside the lens, a single small stylized coffee bean. The documents have a few simple raised lines, no letters or financial charts.
3. Reliable Communication: two overlapping conversation bubbles with a few restrained message lines or dots.
Composition: wide landscape triptych, three separate objects evenly spaced and fully visible, generous negative space between them, all on the same baseline. Consistent front three-quarter camera with a very slight top view. No pedestals, no containers, no panels, no dividers.
Palette: the existing website's deep navy #072340, muted blue #146b91, powder blue #D2E7F2, ivory and white. Restrained premium B2B mood.
Scene/backdrop: seamless very pale cool gray-white background #F5F8FA with soft grounding shadows.
Text: none. No words, labels, numbers, logos, borders, watermarks, people, hands, coins, upward arrows, trading terminals, airplanes, or ships.
Output intent: a sophisticated website illustration style study; clear silhouettes that would remain readable at 96–120 pixels each.
Style/medium: refined dimensional 3D product illustration, matte porcelain and softly sandblasted ceramic. Smooth solid forms with precise, modest bevels; elegant, substantial forms with a little rounded softness. Navy structural elements, ivory faces, powder-blue secondary surfaces. Quiet realistic surface texture, very subtle ambient occlusion, soft studio key light from upper left, delicate contact shadows. This should feel like a high-end corporate visual identity, not a cute toy or inflated clay mascot. The material and dimensional treatment must be consistent across all three objects.
```
